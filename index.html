My 9 Policies

1. Anidaso Editorial Policy

The Editorial Board will work closely with Secretaries and PROs in the various colleges to document and report all academic and association activities.

2. Anidaso Academic Archive & Resource Policy

QR/scan codes will be placed in college libraries to give students easy access to past questions and approved project work samples, promoting independent learning and academic excellence. An academic archive website will be developed where past questions, academic materials, and learning resources are systematically uploaded and preserved.

3. Digital System Policy

A centralized digital system will be created where all minutes, reports, and official documents are uploaded onto a dedicated website for transparency, continuity, and easy reference

4. Anidaso Alumni Engagement Policy

A dedicated alumni page will be created to connect alumni with students, allowing them to offer mentorship, career guidance, and academic support.

5. Anidaso One Campus, One Entrepreneurship Hub

This policy proposes creating a single, modern campus combined with an entrepreneurship hub where trainees can learn practical skills, start businesses, and get support like funding, mentorship, and training.

6. Anidaso Digital Literacy & AI Policy

Digital literacy programs such as Secretary Connect will be sustained to equip students with essential ICT, documentation, and digital communication skills.Inclusive IT and AI Seminars InitiativeTo equip all trainees both men and women with practical skills in Information Technology and Artificial Intelligence, positioning them for opportunities in the digital economy.

7. Anidaso Communication & Confidence Policy

A suggestion and feedback page will be created to allow members to share concerns, ideas, and evaluations on the association’s activities for continuous improvement.

8. Anidaso Library Inclusion & Book Bank

The nine (9) demonstration schools will be registered on the Ghana Library Authority roll to gain access to storybooks, textbooks, and BECE past questions. A Book Bank will be established to support primary school learners in the nine demonstration schools by providing basic learning materials.

9. Anidaso Inclusive Participation Policy (Visual & Hearing Impairments)

Students with disabilities will be fully involved in General Assemblies and TTAG programs, with interpreters or support persons provided to ensure effective participation.
